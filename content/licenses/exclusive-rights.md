## 8. Publishing split
The musical composition of the New Song is shared as follows: Producer owns and is entitled to **50% of the writer's share**, and Licensee (together with any co-writers Licensee brings in) owns the other 50%. The publisher's share follows the same split, with Producer's portion held by Producer or Producer's publishing designee. Producer collects Producer's share directly. This split also applies to any sync fees for the composition.

Licensee keeps 100% of the income from the master recording of the New Song, including master-side sync fees.
